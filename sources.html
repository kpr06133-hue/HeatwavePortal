Data Sources & Stakeholders

The Heatwave Intelligence System relies on information from trusted meteorological and disaster-management organisations to support monitoring, forecasting and public awareness.

🌤 Weather Data

Temperature and weather information can be referred to through the India Meteorological Department (IMD), India's official meteorological organisation.

Visit IMD →

🔥 Heatwave Alerts

Heatwave warnings and weather advisories help identify regions experiencing or expected to experience extreme heat conditions.

📊 Forecast Information

Forecast information helps the system communicate expected temperature conditions and potential heatwave risks for different regions.

View Forecast →

🩺 Safety Advisories

Public safety recommendations focus on hydration, avoiding prolonged exposure to extreme heat and following official heatwave warnings.

🚨 Disaster Management

Disaster-management information supports preparedness, emergency response and awareness during extreme weather events.
