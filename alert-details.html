Severe Heatwave Alert Bulletin

Heatwave Intelligence System

Alert Summary

A Severe Heatwave Warning has been issued for the Mumbai Metropolitan Region due to continuously rising temperatures and prolonged dry weather conditions.

Citizens are advised to stay hydrated, avoid direct sunlight during afternoon hours, and follow official safety advisories.

The recorded maximum temperature is 47°C, which is significantly above the seasonal average.

Bulletin Version : 2.1

This alert has been issued by the IMD using data received from AWS and regional monitoring centres.

Alert Classification : HW / SHW

Note: Forecast values are subject to change based on updated meteorological observations. Citizens are encouraged to follow official weather bulletins for the latest information.
