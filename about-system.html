About the Heatwave Intelligence Portal

About the System

The Heatwave Intelligence System is a front-end web portal designed to present climate and heatwave information in a simple and accessible format. It brings together temperature monitoring, heatwave alerts, forecasts, hotspot information and safety advisories in one place.

What the Portal Provides

Explore the major features of the Heatwave Intelligence System below.

🌡️

Temperature Monitoring

Displays region-wise temperature information to help identify areas experiencing unusually high temperatures.

🔥

Heatwave Alerts

Provides information about heatwave conditions and highlights regions requiring immediate attention.

📊

Forecast Updates

Presents forecast information to help users understand expected temperature conditions across monitored regions.

🗺️

Hotspot Mapping

Helps visualise heatwave-prone regions through an interactive regional hotspot map.

🩺

Safety Advisories

Provides simple precautions and awareness information to help people stay safe during extreme heat.

🚨

Early Warning

Supports early awareness by presenting important alerts and information before heat conditions become severe.
